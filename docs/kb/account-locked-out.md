Your account locks for 15 minutes after 10 wrong passwords in a row. It then unlocks by itself.

## Locked once

Wait 15 minutes, or call the service desk and we will unlock it straight away after checking who
you are.

## Locks again and again, especially after a password change

Something is still trying your **old** password. Nine times out of ten it is a phone:

1. On your phone, open **Settings** > **Mail** (or Outlook) and remove your council account, then
   add it again with the new password.
2. Sign out of **Teams** on your phone and sign back in.
3. If you use a second computer, sign out of it and back in, and disconnect any mapped drives.

If it still locks, call us. We can see which device is sending the wrong password (the domain
controller records it) and tell you exactly what to fix.

This is a known error with a permanent fix in progress: see **Known error: lockouts after a
password change**.
