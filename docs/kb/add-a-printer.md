Council printers use follow-me printing: print to **Kestrel-Print** from anywhere, then tap your ID
badge on any printer in any office to collect it.

## Kestrel-Print is missing

1. Open **Settings** > **Bluetooth and devices** > **Printers and scanners**.
2. Choose **Add device**, wait for **Kestrel-Print** to appear and choose **Add**.

## Nothing comes out

1. Check you tapped your badge on the printer and chose **Print all**.
2. On your computer, open the print queue (Settings > Printers > Kestrel-Print > **Open print
   queue**), cancel everything and print again.
3. Restart your computer.

Still stuck, or the printer shows an error or a paper jam you cannot clear? Log it with the
printer's asset number (on the sticker at the front) and its location.
