title: When someone leaves: what happens to their account
category: Starters, leavers and access

Line managers: tell us as soon as you know, using **Request something** > **People and access** >
**Leaver**. No approval is needed: removing access is never delayed.

## What happens

- **17:30 on the last working day**: the account is disabled, removed from every group and moved to
  the Leavers area. Their password stops working everywhere, including email on phones.
- **30 days later**: the account is deleted. Ask before then if anything still needs to be copied.

## Before they go

- Ask them to move team files from their H drive to the department shared drive.
- Tell us in the request about shared mailboxes or files to hand over.
- Collect their laptop, phone, ID badge and any tokens, and return the devices to IT.

If someone leaves suddenly or in difficult circumstances, call the service desk: we can disable the
account immediately.
