---
title: Lost or stolen laptop or phone
category: Security
keywords: lost laptop, stolen phone, lost device, theft, wipe, data breach
---
Call the service desk **straight away** on **01234 567 890**, day or night. Out of hours, the
on-call engineer is paged.

## What we do

1. Disable sign-in on the device and sign your account out everywhere.
2. Lock or wipe the device remotely through Intune. Council laptops are encrypted with BitLocker, so
   the data on the disk cannot be read without your sign-in.
3. Log it as a high-priority security incident. If personal data might be at risk, the Data
   Protection Officer is told the same day: the council has 72 hours to decide whether to report it
   to the Information Commissioner's Office.

## What you do

- If it was stolen, report it to the police and give us the crime reference number.
- Tell your line manager.

A replacement laptop is normally ready the next working day.
