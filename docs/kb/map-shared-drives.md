When you sign in to a council computer you get:

- **H:** your home drive, for your own work files. Only you (and IT, when asked) can open it.
- **Your department's shared drive**, for the team's files.

## A drive is missing

1. Make sure you are in a council office or connected to the VPN.
2. Restart the computer, or open a command prompt and type `gpupdate /force`, then sign out and in.
3. Still missing? Call the service desk.

## You need another department's drive

Ask for it in the self-service portal: **Request something** > **People and access** >
**Shared drive access**. The drive's data owner approves it, then it is set up automatically within a
few minutes. It works from your next sign-in.

Drives holding special category data (adult social care, children and families, HR and legal) are
only opened for a clear business reason, and access is reviewed every quarter.
