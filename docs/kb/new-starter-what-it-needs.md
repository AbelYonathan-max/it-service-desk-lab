Line managers: ask for a new starter's account as soon as HR confirms the start date, and at least
**3 working days** before they start.

## How to ask

Self-service portal: **Request something** > **People and access** > **New starter**. You need their
name, employee ID (from HR), job title, department, office, start date and contract type.

## What happens

1. You approve the request (it is recorded automatically if you raised it yourself).
2. The account is created within minutes: email address, H drive, department shared drive and
   Microsoft 365 licence. It stays switched off until the start date.
3. At 07:00 on the start date it is switched on.
4. The temporary password is kept securely. Call the service desk on the morning they start: we
   check who you are and read it to you. It must be changed at first sign-in.

## Temporary staff and contractors

Give an end date. The account switches itself off at the end of that day.
