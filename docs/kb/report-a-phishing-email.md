A phishing email tries to get you to click a link, open an attachment, enter your password or make a
payment. Signs: urgency, an unexpected invoice, a sender address that does not quite match, a request
to "verify your account".

## You have not clicked anything

In Outlook, select the email and choose **Report** > **Report phishing**. That's it: it goes to the
security team and is removed from your inbox.

## You clicked a link, opened an attachment or entered your password

Call the service desk **now** on **01234 567 890**. Do not wait, and do not worry about being
blamed: the faster we know, the less damage it can do. We will reset your password, sign you out
everywhere and check your computer. This is logged as a high-priority security incident.

## Finance and payroll staff

Never change bank details or make an urgent payment because of an email alone. Phone the supplier
or colleague on a number you already have.
