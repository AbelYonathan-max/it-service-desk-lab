**Forgotten your password, or told it has expired?** You can reset it yourself in about two minutes,
from any device, at any time.

## Reset it yourself

1. Go to <https://aka.ms/sspr> on your phone or another computer.
2. Enter your council email address and the characters shown.
3. Approve the request in Microsoft Authenticator (or enter the code sent to your phone).
4. Choose a new password: at least 14 characters. Three random words work well, for example
   `Kettle-Orchid-Summit`.
5. Sign in to your council laptop with the new password while it is on the office network or
   connected to the VPN, so it learns the new password.

## If that does not work

Call the service desk on **01234 567 890** (Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00). We will check who
you are before resetting it: expect questions only you can answer, never a request for your current
password. We set a temporary password that you must change when you next sign in.

## After any password change

Update the password everywhere it is saved: your phone's mail app, Teams on your phone, and any
mapped drive on a second computer. Old passwords saved on a phone are the most common reason for an
account locking itself. See **Your account keeps locking**.
