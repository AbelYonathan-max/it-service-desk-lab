Every council account uses multi-factor authentication: a password plus approval on your phone.

## First time

1. Install **Microsoft Authenticator** from your phone's app store.
2. On a computer, go to <https://aka.ms/mfasetup> and sign in with your council account.
3. Choose **Add sign-in method** > **Authenticator app**, then scan the QR code with the app.
4. Approve the test notification.

## New phone

Set up the app on the new phone **before** you reset or hand in the old one: go to
<https://aka.ms/mfasetup>, add the new phone, then delete the old one from the list.

Lost the old phone already? Call the service desk. We will check who you are and give you a
temporary access pass so you can add the new phone.

## Never approve a sign-in you did not start

If your phone asks you to approve a sign-in and you are not signing in, choose **Deny** and report
it as a security incident straight away: someone has your password.
