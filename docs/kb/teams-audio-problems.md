title: Teams calls: people cannot hear you, or you cannot hear them
category: Microsoft 365 and Teams

---
## Fix it during the call

1. Select **More** (...) > **Settings** > **Device settings** and check the right headset is chosen
   for both speaker and microphone.
2. Unplug the headset, wait five seconds and plug it in again, then choose it again in Device
   settings.
3. Check you are not muted, in Teams or on the headset's own button.

## Fix it for next time

- Make a test call: **Settings** > **Devices** > **Make a test call**.
- Restart Teams completely (right-click its icon by the clock > **Quit**).
- Choppy or robotic audio usually means a poor connection. On Wi-Fi at home, move closer to the
  router or use a cable.

If it happens to everyone in one office at the same time, call the service desk: it may be a network
problem we need to know about quickly.
